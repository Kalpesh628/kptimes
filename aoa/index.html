How to pass — for exam tension

Minimum study · target 30+ / 60

Breathe. You do not need all 6 units, and you do not need 60 out of 60. Passing lives around 27–30 marks out of 60. The plan below puts 30 marks on the table from just Q1 plus Q2 — so even with mistakes, you stay above the pass band. Add one more full question and it becomes 45 marks on the table.

Step 1 — the minimum set (learn these first, in this order)

LearnWhere it paysMarks on the table
Q1 — pick five shortsRecurrence · Knapsack difference · LCS short · Branch & Bound · KMP · Divide & Conquer15
Q2Sorting + Merge Sort (fixed in Q2b)15
Total on the table (minimum set)30

Step 2 — the safe set (add this and you cover 45 marks)

One more full question — Q4 (knapsack/LCS) or Q5 (multistage/backtracking) (15) = 15 more marks. 30 + 15 = 45 out of 60, the distinction line.

Step 3 — how to attempt in the hall (2 hours)

Q1 (25 min) → Q2 (30) → strongest remaining (30) → check (15). Draw each graph once, neatly, and label it — redraws eat the paper.

Honest note, not a guarantee. Pattern from only 2 papers, and AOA rotates topics across slots, so learn the topic, not the slot. It raises your odds; it is not a guarantee.
