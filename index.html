Unit I — Linear Algebra

Rank 1 · 8 questions

Eigenvalues run this unit. Every paper asks them twice: once as a 3-mark short, once as a full 6-mark question.

Eigenvalues & eigenvectors

2 / 2 papers min 3 · max 6 marks
Dec 2025 · Q1a · 3 marks
If A = [[3, 2, 1], [0, −1, 4], [0, 0, −2]], find eigenvalues of A⁻¹ + 6I.
Dec 2025 · Q3c · 6 marks
Find eigenvalues and eigenvectors of A = [[3, −1, 1], [−1, 5, −1], [1, −1, 3]].
May 2026 · Q1a · 3 marks
Find the sum and product of the eigenvalues of A = [[−2, −9, 5], [−5, −10, 7], [−9, −21, 14]]. (Shortcut: sum = trace, product = determinant.)
May 2026 · Q3c · 6 marks
Find eigenvalues and eigenvectors of A = [[−9, 4, 4], [−8, 3, 4], [−16, 8, 7]].

Diagonalisation

May 2026 · Q4c · 6 marks

Unit II — LPP & NLPP

May 2026 · Q6c · 6 marks

Solving an LPP (Simplex method)

May 2026 · Q5b · 5 marks
Maximise Z = 7x₁ + 5x₂, subject to −x₁ − 2x₂ ≥ −6, 4x₁ + 3x₂ ≤ 12, x₁, x₂ ≥ 0.
