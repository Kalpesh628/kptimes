The format warning — read this before studying

Dec 2025: Q1 any three × 5 = 15, + any three of Q2–Q6 (15 each), Q6 itself any three × 5 = 15. Total 60, 2 hours.
May 2026: Q1 any five × 2 = 10, + any two of Q2–Q5 (10 each, 5 + 5). Total 30, 1 hour.
If Winter 2026 returns to the 60-mark ESE format, the Dec paper is the template. If it stays 30 marks, the May paper is. Do not study one format's marks as if they were the other's. The tracker below scales topic coverage to the 60-mark scale as an estimate only.

The pattern — topics repeat, not slots

Gen AI has no slot map: the same topics reappear in different questions and different sizes. The repeaters: GPT architecture is asked three times (Dec Q4b 7, May Q2b 5, May Q5b 5 — the two May ones are the same question, attempt only one), CNN twice, RNN twice, GPT variants twice, ethics twice, LLM twice, pre-training twice. Six topics — GenAI definition, GPT architecture, GPT variants, CNN, RNN, ethics — cover questions in both papers.

3 a) [7] What is the multiread mechanism in GPT? b) [8] Explain the architecture of RNN & how it works?

4 a) [8] What are the challenges in Generative AI? b) [7] Write a short note on the architecture and working of GPT models.

5 a) [7] What is chat-GPT and how does it work? b) [8] What is LLM and Types of LLM explain?
